Seth Douglas and Nidhal Mghirbi. Version 1.2.0, November 2026. A preprint; it has not been peer reviewed.

This version replaces the earlier Lean 4.34.1 package by a source-only Lean core, which proves the printed sharp rank inequality, the general quantitative obstruction, both residual-word conclusions, line-subgroup freeness and rank collapse, with existential forms of Theorem 1.1 and of the 1/(6rq) result; lean/README.md and Appendix B give the exact scope.

The manuscript's mathematical statements, equations and existing section numbers are unchanged.

The former Lean 4.34.1 package remains available with version 1.1.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23374284.
